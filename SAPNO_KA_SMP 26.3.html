How to Join

  1. Open Minecraft and choose the version listed on our Discord (Java Edition).
  2. Go to Multiplayer and click Add Server.
  3. Paste the server address play.sapnokasmp.net into the Server Address field.
  4. Click Join Server and read the welcome message. Have fun and build your dream!

Bedrock players (phone, console, Windows 10/11): use the same address with port 19132 if prompted.

Server Rules

  • 1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, or toxic behavior towards any player or staff.
  • 2. No griefing or stealing. Do not destroy, steal from, or build inside another player's claim.
  • 3. No hacks or cheats. Mods that give an unfair advantage, X-ray and macro clients are not allowed.
  • 4. No advertising. Do not promote other servers, channels or websites in chat.
  • 5. Keep chat clean. No spam, caps abuse or inappropriate language.
  • 6. Listen to staff. Staff decisions are final. Appeal politely on Discord if needed.
